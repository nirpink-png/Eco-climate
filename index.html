How to Use EcoClimate

🔍 Search: Type any city name in the top search bar and click search.

📍 GPS Location: Click the target icon next to search to locate yourself automatically.

🎙️ Voice Commands: Tap the green mic button and speak:

  • "Spanish" or "Hindi" (changes language)
  • "Air Quality" (scrolls to AQI section)
  • "Forecast" (scrolls to forecast section)
  • "Dark Mode" (toggles theme)
